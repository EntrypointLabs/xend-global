# Prydd Store

## Register

brand

## Purpose

A real, working demo store for Pay with Prydd, served at shop.prydd.co. It sells Prydd merch and objects for real USDC on Solana, so the founder can hand anyone a phone or laptop and show a payment end to end. Under $100 the shopper confirms with one passkey; over $100 the Prydd app on their phone asks for approval.

## Users

People seeing Prydd for the first time in a demo (investors, merchants, press), and Prydd users buying merch. They arrive curious and leave having paid.

## Brand personality

Monochrome, calm, precise. Black and white only (no brand accent color yet), Montserrat Medium, the sharp Passage symbol, and the sculpted metallic symbol on large surfaces. Objects presented like a printed catalogue issue.

## Anti-references

Stock-photo merch shops, crypto-neon, gradient fintech, Shopify default themes.

## Principles

- The Prydd button is the hero of checkout, Apple Pay-level polish.
- Never claim a payment before the server confirmed it.
- Copy is plain and specific; no em dashes.
